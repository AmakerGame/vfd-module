# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all. We expect all members to be respectful and professional.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful to all contributors
- Be inclusive and welcoming
- Be constructive in feedback

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment or discrimination
- Abuse or threatening language
- Spam or trolling

## Contact

Report violations to the maintainers.
